# Voice-to-Text Feature Guide

The Affiliate Deals Hub now supports voice input for chat, making it even easier to find the best deals and offers!

## How to Use Voice Input

1. **Activate Voice Input**: Click the microphone icon in the chat input area or press `Ctrl+Shift+V` when your cursor is in the message box.

2. **Start Speaking**: When the microphone turns red and pulses, it's listening. Speak clearly and at a normal pace.

3. **Automatic Sending**: When you finish speaking and pause, your message will be processed and sent automatically.

4. **Manual Stop**: You can click the microphone again at any time to stop recording.

## Voice Input Tips

- **Speak Clearly**: Pronounce your words clearly for the best recognition.
- **Use Natural Language**: Speak naturally as if you were typing your query.
- **Short Pauses**: Short pauses between sentences help with recognition.
- **Auto-Timeout**: If no speech is detected for 10 seconds, recording will stop automatically.

## Example Voice Commands

Try these example voice commands:

- "Show me the best electronics deals"
- "Find discounts for Nike shoes"
- "What are the current offers for travel websites"
- "Compare prices for laptops"
- "Show me food delivery coupons"

## Browser Compatibility

Voice recognition works best in modern browsers:
- Chrome (recommended)
- Edge
- Firefox
- Safari (partial support)

Some browsers may require permission to use your microphone the first time you try this feature.

## Keyboard Shortcut

- **Ctrl+Shift+V**: Toggle voice input mode
